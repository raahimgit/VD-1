03.FAQ

[VD‒FAQ] (10)Questions

Quick answers.

Short and clear responses to the questions we hear most often.

Still have questions?

Let’s talk — we’ll clear everything up.

Let's clear it up
01 / 10

Sign in to your account, start a new order, describe the job (size, fabric, placement) and upload your artwork. Orders placed through the site go straight to one of our digitizers, which is quicker and tidier than sending files by email.

Vector files such as AI or EPS give the cleanest results, but JPG, PNG, PDF, PSD and CDR files work too. Rough sketches are welcome, and so are photos of an existing garment you want us to recreate. If a large file won't upload, shrink it or email it to us and we'll take it from there.

Clean artwork is normally turned around within about a day. Detailed or complex designs can take longer, and if you're up against a deadline just tell us when you place the order and we'll see what we can do.

All the major machine formats, including DST, PES, EXP, JEF and VP3, among others. Tell us which machine you run when you order and we'll supply the file in the right format.

Yes, as long as we know what it's going on. Caps, fleece, towels, polos and jackets all behave differently, so we adjust underlay, density and pull compensation for the fabric you name. Please mention it with every order.

Every file is built by an experienced digitizer to your specification and checked before it reaches you, so it runs smoothly with clean edges and as few thread breaks as possible.

Just send us your notes, from your account or by email, plus a photo of the stitch-out if you have one. We'll update the file until it sews the way you expect.

If you order regularly or in volume, get in touch. We're happy to talk through volume pricing that suits the way you work.

Major credit and debit cards and PayPal, all processed securely at checkout.

Use the contact form, send an email, or give us a call during working hours. Messages sent outside those hours are answered as soon as we're back.
